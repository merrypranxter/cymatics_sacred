# The Square Plate: Fourfold Earth

## Context

The square plate is the archetype of **terrestrial geometry**. Four sides, four corners, four cardinal directions. When you vibrate a square plate at its fundamental mode (2,2), the sand traces a cross — the axis mundi, the meeting of heaven and earth.

The ancient Egyptians aligned temples to the cardinal directions not merely astronomically, but acoustically: the fourfold symmetry of sound mirrors the fourfold symmetry of the cosmos.

## Driving Frequency

**432 Hz** — chosen for its relationship to the Earth's Schumann resonance series and its position in ancient tuning systems. The A4 = 432 Hz standard (versus modern 440 Hz) places all notes in simple integer ratios with the Earth's ~7.83 Hz fundamental resonance.

```
432 / 7.83 ≈ 55.2 ≈ 55 = 440/8
```

At 432 Hz, the square plate's (2,2) mode produces the equal-armed cross. At 864 Hz (octave) the cross doubles. At 1296 Hz the pattern begins its journey toward the 9-pointed grid — completion.

## Using the Shader

The `shaders/_chladni_square.frag` shader simulates simultaneous multi-mode forcing. The beating between adjacent modes (driven at slightly different amplitudes) causes the pattern to *breathe* — the sand appears to migrate and reorganize in real time.

Set `u_time` to 0.0 to freeze the fundamental (2,2) mode in isolation.
